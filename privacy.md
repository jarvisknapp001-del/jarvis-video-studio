# JARVIS Video Studio — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 21, 2026**

## 1. Overview

JARVIS Video Studio is a desktop video creation application that helps users create, edit, assemble, preview, and publish original short-form video content.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when JARVIS Video Studio connects to third-party services such as TikTok.

## 2. TikTok Information

When a user chooses to connect a TikTok account, JARVIS Video Studio may receive information authorized through TikTok's official APIs, including:

- TikTok user identification information necessary for account authorization.
- Basic profile information made available through the authorized TikTok scope.
- Authorization and refresh tokens necessary to maintain the connection.
- Video upload and publishing status information.
- Information required to determine available TikTok posting and privacy options.

JARVIS Video Studio does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

## 3. How TikTok Information Is Used

TikTok information is used only to provide functionality requested by the user, including:

- Connecting the user's TikTok account.
- Displaying the connected account.
- Uploading videos to TikTok as drafts.
- Publishing videos when explicitly requested by the user.
- Checking upload and publishing status.
- Displaying posting options made available by TikTok.

JARVIS Video Studio does not automatically publish videos without an explicit user action.

## 4. Local Storage

JARVIS Video Studio is designed primarily as a locally operated desktop application.

Application data, projects, generated media, settings, and publishing history may be stored locally on the user's computer.

TikTok access credentials are handled by the application for authorized API access and are not intentionally displayed in the user interface or publishing history.

## 5. Sharing of Information

JARVIS Video Studio does not sell users' personal information.

Information is transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform actions explicitly requested by the user, such as authorization, video upload, draft creation, direct posting, or status checks.

Other third-party services connected by the user may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## 6. Video and Content Data

Videos, captions, hashtags, posting settings, and related information selected for TikTok submission are transmitted to TikTok when the user initiates the applicable upload or publishing action.

Content that remains local and is not submitted to a third-party service remains under the user's control on their computer, subject to any other services the user chooses to use.

## 7. AI-Generated Content

JARVIS Video Studio may create or process AI-generated or AI-assisted content. Information about whether content is AI-generated may be provided to TikTok when required or selected by the user as part of the publishing process.

## 8. Data Retention

Local JARVIS data remains on the user's computer until it is removed by the user or by application maintenance processes.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies after information has been submitted to them.

## 9. Account Disconnection

Users may disconnect their TikTok account from JARVIS Video Studio.

Users may also revoke authorization through TikTok where supported.

Disconnecting an account prevents JARVIS Video Studio from using that authorization for future TikTok requests unless the account is authorized again.

## 10. Security

JARVIS Video Studio uses reasonable technical measures intended to protect locally stored authorization information and prevent sensitive credentials from being unnecessarily exposed through the user interface or application logs.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Children's Privacy

JARVIS Video Studio is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through its TikTok integration.

Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own age requirements and policies.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as JARVIS Video Studio develops or as third-party API requirements change.

The effective date shown above identifies the current version.

## 13. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or JARVIS Video Studio may be submitted through the project's official public repository.
